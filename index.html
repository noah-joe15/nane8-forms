Lengo la DodosoPurpose of this survey

Karibu kwenye Dodoso la Washiriki wa Maonesho ya NanenaneWelcome to the Nanenane Exhibition Participant Survey

Tunakusanya taarifa kuhusu hali ya biashara, uzalishaji, masoko, usajili wa bidhaa za Made in Tanzania, changamoto na utayari wa kushiriki Maonesho ya 51 ya Biashara ya Kimataifa ya Dar es Salaam (51st DITF). Itachukua takribani dakika 5. We are collecting information about the state of your business, production, markets, Made in Tanzania product registration, challenges, and readiness to take part in the 51st Dar es Salaam International Trade Fair (51st DITF). This takes about 5 minutes.
